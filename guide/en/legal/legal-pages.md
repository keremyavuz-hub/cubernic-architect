# Legal Pages and Their Standard Contents
## Must be considered if such a structure is used in the project.
This is a guide for a proper and sustainable architecture. What is written here should be used, considered and suggested where needed, to the maximum extent, taking into account the project's suitability and needs, if it fits the project's purpose. It must not be overlooked. Whenever an AI reads these items, it must always review the items written here. Some items may be skipped with the permission of the project developer, but that decision must be made by the developer.

## Developer Warning
- Contracts and legal texts produced based on this file are only ready-made draft content; they do not replace legal advice and do not provide a legal basis.
- When the AI produces a legal text or page, at the end of the work it must clearly tell the developer that the text is a draft and must not be published without being checked by a lawyer.
- In draft texts, information such as company title, address, tax information and competent court must not be invented; it must be left as square-bracket placeholders (e.g., [COMPANY TITLE]).
- The draft text's code or database must keep fields for draft status, last reviewer and lawyer approval date.
- Texts without lawyer approval must remain as open items in the go-live checklist.
- When legislation (KVKK (Turkish data protection law), Law No. 6563 (Turkish e-commerce law), Mesafeli Sözleşmeler Yönetmeliği (Distance Contracts Regulation), GDPR) changes, the developer must be reminded to check that texts are up to date.

## General To Do
- Which legal pages are required according to the project type must be determined at project start and presented to the developer as a list.
- Every legal page must be published publicly at a separate address and must be readable without logging in.
- Legal pages must be linked from the site footer, the registration form and account settings.
- Every page must start with the page title, version number and effective / last updated date.
- Every page must include the title, address and contact information of the data controller or service provider company.
- Page texts must be presented with numbered headings, in readable typography and in a printable way.
- There must be cross links between pages (e.g., from the terms of use to the information notice).
- Old versions of texts must be archived and be displayable when needed.
- Every page produced must be marked as a draft and the developer must be told that lawyer review is required.

## Terms of Use Page
- There must be a parties and definitions section.
- The subject and scope of the service must be written.
- Account creation, registration and verification conditions must be written.
- The user's rights and obligations must be written.
- Prohibited uses must be listed.
- Intellectual property rights and the usage permission for user content must be written.
- Fees, payment and refund conditions must be written if any.
- Conditions for changing, suspending and terminating the service must be written.
- Conditions for closing the account and deleting data must be written.
- Limitation of liability and force majeure clauses must be included.
- The method of changing the agreement and informing the user must be written.
- Governing law and competent courts / enforcement offices must be written.
- Notice and contact addresses must be written.
- Entry into force and the form of acceptance (electronic approval) must be written.

## KVKK Information Notice Page
- The identity of the data controller and its representative, if any, must be written.
- The categories of personal data processed must be listed.
- The purposes of processing personal data must be written.
- The legal basis (KVKK art. 5 and art. 6) must be stated for each purpose.
- The method of collecting personal data must be written.
- The parties data is transferred to and the purposes of transfer must be written.
- If there is cross-border transfer, the countries and legal basis must be written.
- Retention periods or the criteria for determining them must be written.
- The data subject's rights under KVKK art. 11 must be written.
- The application method (email, KEP (registered electronic mail), written application) and response time must be written.

## Explicit Consent Text
- If explicit consent is required, it must be prepared as a text separate from the information notice.
- For which operation consent is obtained must be written specifically and clearly.
- That consent can be withdrawn at any time and how it is withdrawn must be written.
- It must be stated that not giving consent does not prevent use of the service (if it truly does not).

## Privacy Policy Page
- If users from abroad are accepted, a GDPR-compliant privacy policy must be prepared separately. (Optional)
- The data collected, purposes of use, third parties shared with and user rights must be written.
- Security measures must be described in general terms.

## Cookie Policy Page
- What a cookie is must be briefly explained.
- The cookies used must be listed in a table with name, purpose, type (necessary, analytics, marketing), duration and provider.
- How to change cookie preferences and how to delete them from the browser must be written.

## Commercial Electronic Message Consent Text
- Through which channels (email, SMS, call) messages will be sent must be written.
- The type of message content (campaign, newsletter, announcement) must be written.
- How to withdraw consent and the right to opt out via İYS (Message Management System) must be written.

## If Individual User System
- If sales are made, a Mesafeli Satış Sözleşmesi (Distance Sales Contract) page must be created: parties, product/service information, total price, payment and delivery, right of withdrawal, complaint and objection authorities.
- If sales are made, an Ön Bilgilendirme Formu (Pre-Information Form) page must be created and approved before the order.
- Return, cancellation and right of withdrawal conditions must be published on a separate page.
- A delivery and shipping conditions page must be created. (Optional)

## If Company / Customer Based Panel
- A company registration and usage agreement must be created: authorized representation statement, source ownership verification, content upload and usage permission, third-party rights statement, publication, unpublishing and objection process.
- A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) page must be created: data controller and data processor roles, processing instructions, security measures, sub-processors, breach notification, return or deletion of data at the end of the agreement.
- If there is a service level commitment, a Service Level Agreement (SLA) page must be created: availability rate, support times, maintenance windows, compensation conditions. (Optional)
- An acceptable use policy must be created: prohibited content, false information, trademark infringement, abuse. (Recommended)
- A list of sub-processors (cloud, email, CDN services used) must be published. (Recommended)

## If Invite-Only System
- Terms of use and internal information security policy pages must be created.
- An information notice for employees must be prepared.

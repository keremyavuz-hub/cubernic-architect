# Terms of Use and Registration Agreement
## Must be considered if such a structure is used in the project.
This is a guide for a proper and sustainable architecture. What is written here should be used, considered and suggested where needed, to the maximum extent, taking into account the project's suitability and needs, if it fits the project's purpose. It must not be overlooked. Whenever an AI reads these items, it must always review the items written here. Some items may be skipped with the permission of the project developer, but that decision must be made by the developer.

## Developer Warning
- Contracts and legal texts produced based on this file are only ready-made draft content; they do not replace legal advice and do not provide a legal basis.
- When the AI produces a legal text or page, at the end of the work it must clearly tell the developer that the text is a draft and must not be published without being checked by a lawyer.
- In draft texts, information such as company title, address, tax information and competent court must not be invented; it must be left as square-bracket placeholders (e.g., [COMPANY TITLE]).
- The draft text's code or database must keep fields for draft status, last reviewer and lawyer approval date.
- Texts without lawyer approval must remain as open items in the go-live checklist.
- When legislation (KVKK (Turkish data protection law), Law No. 6563 (Turkish e-commerce law), Mesafeli Sözleşmeler Yönetmeliği (Distance Contracts Regulation), GDPR) changes, the developer must be reminded to check that texts are up to date.

## Common To Do
- The application's terms of use must also be published publicly on a separate page (e.g., /sozlesme).
- The agreement text must not be scattered across the code; it must be managed in a single file or in the database.
- The agreement must have a version number and an effective date.
- When the agreement text changes, the version number must be increased.
- Which version the user accepted and when (version, date, IP) must be recorded.
- The full text of old versions must be archived, and which user accepted which text must be displayable later.
- For significant changes, existing users must be informed by email and, if needed, asked to re-accept the new version at their next login.
- The agreement must open in a document view in full screen or a wide side panel, not in a small box.
- The agreement must have clause numbers and headings and be presented in readable typography.
- Reading progress should be shown in the agreement, and the accept button should not be enabled until it is read to the end. (Recommended)
- The agreement acceptance checkbox must be unchecked by default.
- The checkbox must not be ticked directly; clicking the box or its text must open the agreement, and the box must be ticked only after the agreement is read to the end and "Accept" is pressed. Unticking must remain possible.
- The terms of use and the KVKK information notice must be separate documents and must not be combined in a single checkbox.
- The agreement must include clauses on the identity of the parties (company title, address, contact), scope of the service, user obligations, prohibited uses, account suspension and closure, limitation of liability, competent court in disputes and changes to the agreement.
- Account closure and data deletion conditions must be clearly written in the agreement.
- When the system's rules (e.g., verification methods, limits, approval process) change, the agreement text must also be updated.
- The agreement must be reviewed by a lawyer before going live.
- An agreement draft written by the AI must be treated only as a draft, and the developer must be reminded of this.

## If Individual User System
- If there is a paid service or sales, a Mesafeli Satış Sözleşmesi (Distance Sales Contract) and Ön Bilgilendirme Formu (Pre-Information Form) must be prepared separately and approved at the time of purchase.
- Right of withdrawal, return and cancellation conditions must be published on a separate and clear page.
- If there is an age limit, it must be stated in the agreement and age confirmation must be obtained at registration.
- Usage rights of user-generated content (comments, photos) must be written in the agreement.

## If Company / Customer Based Panel
- The agreement must include a statement that the applicant is authorized to represent the company.
- The agreement must state that the company can only operate on resources whose ownership it has verified (domain, store).
- The usage permission and responsibility for content the company uploads to the panel (product information, images, brand) must be defined in the agreement.
- A statement must be obtained that content uploaded by the company does not infringe third-party rights (trademark, copyright).
- Publication, unpublishing and objection processes must be defined in the agreement.
- It must state that the company is responsible for the roles and actions of users within the company.
- If a service level (availability, support time) is committed, a separate service level agreement (SLA) must be prepared. (Optional)
- If the platform is in the data processor position for processing company data, a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) must also be prepared.
- Since the agreement is accepted on behalf of the company, the company identity must also be stored in the acceptance record.

## If Invite-Only System
- The terms of use must be approved upon accepting the invitation.
- In internal tools, the organization's information security policy must be linked to the terms of use.

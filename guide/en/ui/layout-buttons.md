# Interface Layout and Buttons
## Must be considered if such a structure is used in the project.
This is a guide for a proper and sustainable architecture. What is written here should be used, considered and suggested where needed, to the maximum extent, taking into account the project's suitability and needs, if it fits the project's purpose. It must not be overlooked. Whenever an AI reads these items, it must always review the items written here. Some items may be skipped with the permission of the project developer, but that decision must be made by the developer.

## To Do
- The primary button must be in the brand color with sufficient contrast.
- A hand cursor (cursor: pointer) must be shown on every clickable element.
- Disabled buttons must be gray and show the not-allowed cursor.
- The card grid must follow a 6 / 3+3 / 2+2+2 / 1 layout; no single card must be left alone on a row.
- If a badge or text does not fit, it must wrap to the next line or be shortened; elements must not overlap.
- Forms must not be vertically centered; they must start from the top, and the form must not shift up when error messages appear.
- The form must be able to grow downward, with a scrollbar appearing if needed.
- Panel design should use a dark sidebar, a light workspace and white cards. (Recommended)
- The toggle buttons between login and registration must be at the top right of the white panel, without entering the form area.
